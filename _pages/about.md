I’m Oliver. I’m a researcher and designer who delivers design research. I bring design to complex social change and sustainability projects that involve tech, policy, services and systems. I use creative and diy methods, games and facilitation in my work, and talk software and data. 

I currently do a patchwork of things between consultancy, academia and DIY:

- 👩‍🎤 [Lancaster University](https://research.lancaster-university.uk/en/persons/oliver-bates/) - research fellow. sustainable futures and digital research infrastructure
- 🌀 [fractals.coop](https://fractals.coop) - co-owner/worker, leading UCD and service evaluation consultancy, creative facilitation, climate futures conversations 
- 📊 [Cetis LLP](https://www.cetis.org.uk) - partner, edutech, data analysis, viz, carbon data 

You can find my work in a bunch of different places:
- 👾 [itch.io](https://oliverbates.itch.io/) - my games, zines and diy stuff  
- ⚙️ [Crank and File](https://crankandfile.co.uk) - gig economy cycle courier provocations and futures  
- 🫖 [Manky Tea Towel Museum](https://ben.kirman.org/stuff/manky-tea-towels/) - contribute your tea towels to the Manky Tea Towel Museum
- 🖥️ [GitHub](http://github.com/oscarechobravo) - a chaotic archive of code    
- 📜 [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.co.uk/citations?user=ZXDbf_EAAAAJ&hl=en) - academic outputs
