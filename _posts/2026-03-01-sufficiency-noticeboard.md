As part of work delivering design futures work on the Sustainable Digital Research Infrastructure project I designed a noticeboard from the future. 

The aim was to produce a diegetic artefact to be deployed in everyday contexts with research organisations supporting or delivering environmental modelling and utilising scientific computing.

This is to provoke discussion about what a sufficient future might look like, and what might need to change in organisational structures and cultures whilst journeying towards that future. 

Workshop told us about the technology, but the big assumption was that an organisation would know what transformation towards a sufficient organisation looks like.
