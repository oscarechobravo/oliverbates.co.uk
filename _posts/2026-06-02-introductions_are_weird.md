Well that was awkward. 

I'm at a moment in my career where I am asking myself questions like *what am I about*, *who am I*, and *how do I introduce myself professionally*. 

I want to introduce myself to you but I don't know who *you* are. You see, I have this impulsion to tweak my "About Me" depending on who you are, who the audience is. I have no idea who you are dear reader I can't really tailor anything. If I am to be myself you're getting the raw feed. Take the [About Me]({{ site.baseurl }}/about) page as a snapshot made for a generic person.
