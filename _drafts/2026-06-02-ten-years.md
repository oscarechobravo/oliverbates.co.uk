## Picking up where I left off
I started writing this post 18 months ago when I last attempted to kick energy into my non academic writing. I have an urge to write for myself in order to get things out of my head and process them. Academic writing alongside other work related writing has blocked me from writing as myself. 

The 18 month old post was a brief yet a complete thought. When reading over I felt the lack of detail and voice. I wanted to go back and change it. So here it is.

## Journeying

For the last two years I have been thinking about personal journeys and personal growth (in the expansion of mind, body and soul). Being in my late 30s, becoming a father, listening to my body, getting a diagnosis, and learning to be myself. It's a lot. 

At ICT4S 2024 I met new people and rekindled old research partnerships leaving me energised. 

I critique normative theories of change in computing and the sustainable futures. Eleven years ago I wrote about the need to do more holistic/systemic work to understand the breadth and depth of ICT's environmental emissions and it's relationship with everyday life. 

I am now older, wiser (?), and ready to admit that back then I couldn't fathom the system that I exist in. I was trying to understand dying or being put to rest. I feel like I am centring my lived experience way more than I was back then, and allowing myself to be conscious of the decay of modernity in ways that I couldn't see before. 

## Decaying and dying systems

Collapse aware. I think I might be collapse aware now. I know that the systems around me are decaying 

It's tough to reckon with a complete change in world view, but I strongly believe that we need to be thinking more about hospicing and endings in the work we do. And of course that comes with a radical rethinking of how we grieve and do endings. There are no complete thoughts right now, it's just something I've been thinking about as I work with friends on the Berkana Two Loops and what that means for our lives and practice. 

Chefurka's Ladder of Awareness (thanks Elina) is a little framework (or tool) for thinking about our levels of awareness when it comes to problems, interconnections, and predicaments.

> Chefurka’s Ladder of Awareness:
> 1. Dead asleep.
> 2. Awareness of one fundamental problem. 
> 3. Awareness of many problems.
> 4. Awareness of the interconnections between the many problems.
> 5. Awareness that the predicament encompasses all aspects of life.

## Endings

In 2014 I was at 2 or maybe 3, and now I am at 5. Before becoming a father I was in a state of denial, not willing to confront that these interconnected predicaments 

Whilst I think it's time to let the system die gracefully there's so much work that can be done to prepare for what comes next.
